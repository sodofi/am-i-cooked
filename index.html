rules of the court

↳ how to keep it chaotic without getting anyone in real trouble.
  • be specific — the gossip is in the details.
  • anonymous by default. names are vibes, not evidence.
  • give us the stakes. why did this matter?
  • include the part you are embarrassed to admit.
  • no doxxing. ever.
  • no real accusations of serious crimes.
  • chaotic is good. defamatory is not.
  • if "everyone told me not to do this" appears — probably cooked.
